# Keeping a reading list that works for you

Keeping a reading list is a good way to stay informed, as long as the list doesn't get out of hand. With some care, a simple collection of links can become a useful resource for learning.

## How Margin works

A reading list doesn't need to be complicated. Margin, our fictional reading-list app, keeps it that way. It stores a title, URL, and category for each link. You can filter by category and bookmark items you want to come back to. Everything stays in your browser, and there's no account or cloud sync to worry about.

## Building better habits

A weekly review helps you keep up with the collection. Remove links you no longer want to read, and keep the ones that connect to a project you are working on. The list stays short enough to use.

The goal is a list that makes good use of your reading time. A small, focused list is more useful than an extensive collection you never open.
