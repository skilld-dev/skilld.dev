# Keep a reading list that works

A pile of saved links only becomes a reading list once you can sort it. The links you keep should be the ones you'll actually return to, and structure is what makes that possible.

## What Margin does

Margin, our fictional reading-list app, stores three things for each link: a title, URL, and category. You can filter by category and bookmark items you want to come back to. Everything stays in your browser; there's no account and no cloud sync, which keeps things simple.

## A weekly review

Go through the list once a week. Remove the links you no longer want to read, and keep the ones tied to a project you're working on. A small, focused list beats a big collection you never open.
