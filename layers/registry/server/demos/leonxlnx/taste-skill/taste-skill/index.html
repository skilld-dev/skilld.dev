    Questions people ask before their first night.

    Can I bring skis or a bike?

    Yes. Every train has a luggage van with ski racks and bike hooks. Add one when you book; it costs €12 per item.

    Are dogs allowed?

    Dogs travel free in single cabins and double suites. Couchettes are kept pet-free for people with allergies.

    What happens if snow closes a pass?

    We hold the train at the last town before the pass, keep the heating on, and serve breakfast aboard. If you arrive more than two hours late, we refund half the fare.

    Is it suitable for children?

    Very. Under-fours ride free in a parent’s berth, and a couchette compartment can be booked as a single family cabin.
