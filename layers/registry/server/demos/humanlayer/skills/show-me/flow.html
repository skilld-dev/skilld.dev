      The happy path above took 42 seconds. When riders keep missing the offer, dispatch escalates in this order.

      1. Next in rankEach rider gets one 30 s offer. A miss passes it to the next rider.
      2. Widen to 4 kmAfter 3 misses, the radius grows and the shortlist and rank run again.
      3. Tell the merchantAt 3 min unassigned, the merchant sees Finding a rider.
      4. Human dispatcherAt 4 min unassigned, a dispatcher picks a rider by hand.
      Cogwhistle is a fictional app. Riders, places, and numbers are made up for this example. Rider distances on the map are to scale.
