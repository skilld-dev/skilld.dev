Brothwheel

Neighborhood soup in glass jars that come back.

A co-op owned by the cooks who make the soup and the neighbors who eat it.

The problem

Good soup takes hours. Its takeout tub lasts one meal.

  • Time

    A proper stock simmers for four hours. Weeknights rarely have four hours.

  • Waste

    Takeout soup comes in a plastic tub that gets used once.

  • Idle kitchens

    Community kitchens sit empty most weekday afternoons.

How it works

One jar goes round and round.

  1. Cook. Member cooks make three soups in a shared kitchen.

  2. Fill. Liter jars, filled hot, labeled by batch and date.

  3. Collect. At our counter, a corner shop shelf, or by cargo bike.

  4. Eat. Best within four days, kept chilled.

  5. Return. Any pickup point takes the jar back and refunds $3.

  6. Wash. A commercial dishwasher sanitizes every jar and lid.

A jar and lid cost us $2.40. The $3 deposit covers any jar that never comes back.
