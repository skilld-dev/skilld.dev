Kiln room rules

This is the sheet taped to the kiln room door. Read it once and every load goes in clean.

Clay that matches the firing

Glaze loads take stoneware and porcelain rated for cone 5 to 7. Bisque loads take any clay body. Earthenware is welcome in bisque but cannot go into a cone 6 glaze firing; it will slump and bloat.

Witness cones sit at three heights in every load. You can see the photos on your pickup email.

Bone dry, or it waits

Greenware for bisque has to be fully dry: room temperature to the touch, no cool spots on the foot. Damp pots crack and can blow out and take a neighbor’s work with them, so we hold them for the next load.

Clean feet, ¼ inch up

Wax or wipe the foot and the bottom quarter inch of every glazed piece. Glaze on a foot fuses to the shelf. Pieces that arrive with glazed feet are set aside, not fired.

Cookies under anything that moves

Runny, crawling or untested glazes sit on a bisque cookie. The studio keeps a bin of them by the intake cart, free.

Mark every piece

Write your hold ticket number on the foot in underglaze pencil. Unmarked work goes to the lost shelf for a month.

Studio glazes or yours

Use the studio’s cone 6 glazes on the glaze bar, or bring commercial ones labeled for cone 5 to 6. No low-fire glazes in a cone 6 load, and nothing containing lead.

Do I need to be a studio member?

No. Anyone can book shelf space for a single firing. Most of our potters throw at home, at a community wheel, or finished a class and lost their kiln access.

What if my pot breaks in the kiln?

Firing is hard on clay and some losses are the clay’s doing, not the kiln’s. If a piece is lost to a firing fault on our side, such as a controller error or a shelf failure, you get that space back free on any later load.

Can I change or cancel a hold?

Yes, from the link in your hold email, up to 24 hours before drop-off closes. After that the space releases to the waitlist automatically.

Do you fire reduction or cone 10?

Not yet. Big Marrow is an electric kiln and fires in oxidation only. Bisque at cone 04 and glaze at cone 6 cover most studio clay bodies and glazes.

How tall can a piece be?

Up to 12 in on the bottom level. For anything taller, book a private load and we’ll post the shelves around your piece.
