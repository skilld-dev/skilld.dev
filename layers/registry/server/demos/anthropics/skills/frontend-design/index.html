You know your spot. Tidepool does the tide math every day so you don't have to.

When is it in range?
Set the lowest and highest tide each break works on. Tidepool marks those hours on every chart, for every day of the year.
Is it pushing or draining?
Some sandbars only work on an incoming tide. Mark a break as rising only and Tidepool leaves the falling hours out of its window.
Can I surf it before work?
First light and sunset sit on the chart. A window that opens in the dark doesn't count, so you only see the hours you can use.
No signal at the car park?
A full year of tide tables downloads with each break. The charts work in airplane mode.
Can it tell me when to go?
Turn on alerts for a break and Tidepool tells you one hour before its window opens.
