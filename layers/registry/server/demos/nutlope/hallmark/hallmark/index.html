What the ledger does, and what it won’t

Mark a chapter
One tap when you finish it. No page counts, no streaks, no reading-speed leaderboard. A chapter is either read or it isn’t.
The line moves on its own
Quillmoor works out the last chapter every member has finished and moves the line there. Nobody has to tell the group they’re behind.
Margin notes wait for you
Notes are pinned to a chapter and stay folded until you’ve marked it. Readers who are ahead can write as much as they like, and nobody else sees it early.
One ledger per book
When the club picks its next title, the old ledger closes and goes into an archive you can look back through, with every note still in place.
