How it works

From CAT log to sent invoice in three steps

Setup takes about 6 minutes. After that, a typical invoice takes under 2.

  1. 01

    Set your rate card once

    Add each language pair, your rate per word or character, and your fuzzy grid. Copy a grid to another client in one click.

  2. 02

    Drop in the analysis log

    Drag the export from Trados Studio, memoQ, or Phrase onto the client. Glossmere reads each match band and drafts the lines.

  3. 03

    Check it and send

    Add a PO number if the client needs one, then send. Clients pay by SEPA transfer or card, and you see the payment the same day.

    Privacy policy

    Last updated 18 September 2026

    What we collect

    Your name, email address, and the business details you add to invoices. For each client, their name, address, and VAT number. From each analysis log, file names and word counts for each match band.

    What we never collect

    Source or target texts. Glossmere reads the analysis log only, and the log holds counts, not content.

    Where your data lives

    On servers in Frankfurt, Germany, encrypted at rest. Payment details go straight to our payment processor and never touch our servers.

    Cookies and tracking

    This page sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It loads the Geist typeface from jsDelivr, which sees your IP address. The app sets one session cookie after you sign in.

    Your rights

    Export or delete your account from settings at any time. For anything else, email privacy@glossmere.app and we reply within 5 working days.
